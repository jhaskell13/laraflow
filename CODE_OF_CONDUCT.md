# Code of Conduct

Laraflow follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

In short: participate in issues, pull requests, discussions, and reviews with respect and in good faith; welcome newcomers; disagree about code, not about people; and accept that maintainers may moderate, edit, or remove contributions and comments that do not meet that bar.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment of any kind, personal or political attacks, publishing others' private information, and sustained disruption of the project's work.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through [GitHub's private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/jhaskell13/laraflow/security/advisories/new) for this repository (the same confidential channel used for security reports) or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially; you will receive an acknowledgement within 7 days.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will respond with the action the Covenant describes for the severity of the behaviour, from a private correction to a permanent ban. Decisions may be appealed to the maintainers in writing.
