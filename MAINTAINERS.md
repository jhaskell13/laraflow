## What maintainers do

- **Review and merge** pull requests. A change to a guarantee needs a decision record ([docs/decisions/](docs/decisions/README.md)) that a maintainer accepts.
- **Cut releases**: convert the changelog, tag, and publish the draft the `Release` workflow prepares ([CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#cutting-a-release)). Publishing is always a person's decision.
- **Handle security reports** privately and within the window in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Keep the community connector list honest** ([docs/community-connectors.md](docs/community-connectors.md)).

## Becoming one

Sustained, reviewed contributions — code, documentation, or triage — over several releases, and agreement from the existing maintainers. A second maintainer is the project's most important open need before 1.0: a single maintainer is a single point of failure for releases and security response.
